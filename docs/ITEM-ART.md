# Shop item art: ChatGPT prompts

50 images: every item in the Shop, plus the 10 businesses. Square, 1024 × 1024. Send them back as
`<file name>.jpg` (the names below); they replace `public/items/<file name>.jpg` one for one.

## Paste this first, once

> I'm making product images for the shop of a noir crime game. Every image uses the same style:
> cinematic painted realism, a rainy night, deep teal shadows and warm amber light, dramatic rim
> lighting, a little film grain. One object, centred, filling about two thirds of a square frame,
> on a dark, softly lit surface (a wet tabletop, a workbench, a pavement), with plain dark space
> around it. Same camera angle every time: slightly above, three-quarter view. No text, no
> letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no people's faces, no hands unless I ask. Make
> them one at a time; here is the first.

Start each one after that with "Same style as the last image:". Businesses and homes are buildings,
so they break the "one object" rule: shoot them from across a wet street at night, the building
centred.
