# Career rank portraits: ChatGPT prompts

Every career has one portrait per rank (76 in all), plus 7 for people without a career. They show
on your own card, the People list, fights and the career ladder. Square, 1024 × 1024, and the
picture must **fill the whole frame** (no border). Name each file `<career>-<rank>.jpg`
(for example `medicine-4.jpg` is the Doctor) and send them in a zip per career.

## Paste this first, once per chat

> I'm making career portraits for a noir crime game: one per rank, from the bottom of a career to
> the top. Same style every time: cinematic painted realism, night, deep teal shadows and warm
> amber light, dramatic rim lighting, a little film grain. Square frame, filled edge to edge, no
> border or empty margin. One person, waist up, centred. Their face is never clearly visible:
> turned away, in silhouette, lit from behind, or hidden by shadow, a hat brim or a mask, so any
> player can picture themselves. The person could be a man or a woman. Show the rank through the
> clothes, the tools and the place, getting richer and more powerful from rank to rank. No text,
> no letters, no logos, no badges with writing. Make them one at a time; here is the first.

Start each one after that with "Same style as the last portrait:". Do one career per chat so the
ranks match each other.
