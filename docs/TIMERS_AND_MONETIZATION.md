# Timers, Smart Pop-ups & Monetization

## 1. Timers (kept from the classics)

| Timer | What it does | How to speed it up (free) |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | Refills over time; jobs cost Energy | Food, sleep at home (better housing = faster), daily bonus |
| **Nerve** | Refills over time; crimes cost Nerve | Certain items, story rewards |
| **Hospital** | Time spent recovering after losing a fight | Medic players, medkits, better clinics |
| **Jail** | Time served after an arrest | Lawyer players, bail paid by your crew, good behavior |
| **Travel** | Time spent travelling between countries | Faster (pricier) transport |
| **Long jobs / training** | Jobs that take time to finish (shifts, studies, heists) | None: these are the "come back later" hook |

Players **wait or play smart**. Nothing about timers is pay-to-win at launch.

## 2. Smart pop-ups that take you to the job

When a timer finishes, the game tells you **and gives a one-tap button that goes
straight to what you can do next**.

**Inside the game (pop-up / toast):**
> ⚡ **Energy full!** You can unload trucks 4 times.  **[Go to job →]**
>
> 🏥 **You're out of the hospital.** The guy who put you there is in Southside.  **[Get revenge →]**
>
> 🚔 **Shift starts in 5 min** at Precinct Square.  **[Clock in →]**

**Outside the game (push notifications on phone and desktop):**
- Uses Web Push, so it works from the web app on Android, desktop, and on iPhone
  when the player has added the game to their home screen (iOS 16.4+).
- Tapping the notification opens the game **directly on that job's screen** (a deep link).

**Recommended next job:** the pop-up suggests the best job for that moment
(based on energy, location and unlocked jobs), so new players always know what to do.

**Respect the player:**
- Settings to turn each type on or off.
- Quiet hours (no notifications at night by default).
- At most a few notifications per day; the important ones (attacked, arrested) first.

## 3. Monetization: none at launch, ready for later

No payments in the first version. We design so it can be added later **without
breaking fairness**. Rule: **never sell stats, protection or wins.**

### Ideas for later (ranked by fairness)

| Idea | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cosmetics:** outfits, avatar parts, profile frames, vigilante costumes | ✅ Best | Fits the layered comic avatars. Zero gameplay effect. |
| **Season pass** with mostly cosmetic rewards | ✅ Good | Free track for everyone, paid track for extras. |
| **Custom profile photo** (paid) | ⚠️ OK with care | See §4. |
| **Convenience:** extra job queue slot, more saved outfits | ⚠️ OK if small | Must not turn into pay-to-win. |
| **Paying to refill energy / skip timers** | ⚠️ Risky | If ever added: daily cap, and the same refills must be earnable in-game. |
| **Buying stats, protection, or game money** | ❌ Never | Breaks the hidden-stat system and the PvP. |

## 4. Paid profile photos — recommendation

Paying to upload your own photo is **acceptable**, but it brings real costs:

- **Moderation:** people will upload offensive, violent or explicit images, or photos
  of other people. You need automatic image screening + a report button + someone to review.
- **Privacy & minors:** real-face photos of young players are a risk. Consider an age
  gate, or blurring faces.
- **Copyright:** people will upload celebrities, logos, cartoon characters.
- **Style:** a real photo looks out of place next to comic-style art.

**Suggested approach:**
1. **Free** at launch: the comic **avatar builder** (face, hair, outfit layers).
2. **Paid later: "Comic Portrait"**, where the player uploads a photo and the game turns it into
   a comic-style drawing that matches the art. It is automatically screened
   before anyone else can see it, and other players can report it.
3. Unlock it through play as well (e.g. reaching a rank), so it isn't paywall-only.
