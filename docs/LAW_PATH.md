# The Law Path — Deep Design

> The Law side is the deepest path in the game: **3 branches, 8 ranks each, clean or
> corrupt variants, and a player-driven justice system** that connects every player
> in the city. It is designed to be balanced against the Street side, not stronger.

---

## 1. Story spine: what "Injustice" means

The whole game hangs on one event, **The Verdict**:

> You had a normal life — a job, an apartment, people who knew your name. Then you were
> **framed** for a crime you didn't commit. A rigged trial. A bought witness. A judge
> who didn't look up. Three years later you walk out of prison with nothing: no money,
> no home, no name. You are a bum on the streets of **Grayhaven**.
>
> Somebody did this to you. Somebody is still doing it to others.

Every path chases the same question — **who framed you, and why** — but answers it differently:

| Path | How you pursue the truth | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Law – Police** | Reopen your own case from inside the system | Justice through order |
| **Law – Courts** | Overturn the verdict; expose the judges and lawyers who sold it | Justice through truth |
| **Law – Vigilante** | The system failed you; you hunt the guilty yourself | Justice outside the law |
| **Street paths** | Get rich and powerful enough that nobody can touch you — then take revenge | Power as justice |

Story is delivered as **short comic-panel chapters** (3–6 panels) unlocked at rank
milestones. Each branch reveals a different piece of the conspiracy, so the full truth is
only seen by players who explore multiple paths (replay value / alt characters).

The ending of each chapter changes based on the hidden **Integrity** stat (clean vs.
corrupt), so two cops can live very different stories.

---

## 2. New hidden stats for the Law side

The Law path adds two stats to the six base stats (Strength, Toughness, Agility, Wits,
Nerve, Charisma). They exist for every player, but matter most here.

| Stat | Grown by | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Perception** | Investigations, patrols, surveillance, reading people | Finding evidence, spotting lies and ambushes, accuracy of your "gut read" on opponents |
| **Integrity** | Moral choices in jobs & story (refusing bribes, honest testimony…) — **can go up or down** | Unlocks clean vs. corrupt jobs, story endings, how NPCs trust you, Internal Affairs risk |

Integrity is the only stat that can drop. It works like a hidden moral compass — the game
hints at it (*"The desk sergeant trusts you with the evidence locker keys now."* /
*"People stop talking when you walk into the room."*).

### Visible meters

| Meter | Who | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Heat** | Everyone | How wanted you are by the law. Criminals build it; law players hunt it. |
| **Suspicion** | Law players | How closely Internal Affairs is watching you. Built by corrupt acts. |
| **Rank** | Law players | Your official title — visible to all. |

---

## 3. Entry: from bum to the right side of the law

Law jobs require a **clean record** (low Heat). Bum-phase entry jobs:

| Entry job | Leads to | Grows |
|---|---|---|
| Night security guard at a warehouse | Police | Toughness, Perception |
| Courthouse janitor / file clerk | Courts | Wits, Perception |
| Neighborhood watch volunteer | Vigilante | Agility, Nerve |

**Second chances:** a player who drifted into crime can still switch to Law by getting
their record **expunged** — a service sold by Lawyer players (or an expensive NPC lawyer).
That creates trade between paths.

## 4. The three branches

### 4.1 Police — "Order"

| Rank | Title | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Academy Cadet | Training jobs, physical exam (assessment, see §7) |
| 2 | Patrol Officer | Patrol jobs, issue fines to low-Heat NPCs, salary |
| 3 | Senior Officer | **Arrest** players with high Heat (needs a warrant) |
| 4 | Detective | **Investigations** — build evidence files on players & NPC gangs |
| 5 | Sergeant | Lead squad operations (co-op raids with other law players) |
| 6 | Lieutenant | Request warrants faster; assign NPC officers |
| 7 | Captain | Control a district precinct; set patrol focus (raises Heat gain in a district) |
| 8 | Commissioner | City-wide operations; seasonal title (one per season, competitive) |

**Specialization at Detective (choose 1):**
- **Narcotics** — raids on Hustler operations, seize goods.
- **Homicide** — big story arcs, chase the people behind The Verdict.
- **Tactical (SWAT)** — combat-focused; best at arresting dangerous Enforcers.
- **Internal Affairs** — hunts **corrupt law players**. The counter to corruption.

### 4.2 Courts — "Truth"

| Rank | Title | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | File Clerk | Paperwork jobs, read case files (lore) |
| 2 | Paralegal | Assist trials, small fees |
| 3 | Law Student | Study jobs (Wits), Bar Exam at the end (assessment) |
| 4 | Junior Attorney | **Choose a side:** Defense or Prosecution |
| 5 | Senior Attorney | Represent players in trials; expunge records (Defense) |
| 6 | Partner / Deputy DA | Run a firm (hire NPC staff) or lead big prosecutions |
| 7 | Judge | **Set sentences** within legal ranges; approve warrants |
| 8 | Chief Justice | Seasonal title; can change one "city law" per season (e.g. longer jail for robbery) |

- **Defense Attorney** — earns from criminals: shorten jail time, get cases dismissed, expunge records.
- **Prosecutor** — earns from convictions: lengthen sentences, seize criminal assets.

### 4.3 Vigilante — "Outside the law"

| Rank | Title | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Watcher | Neighborhood watch jobs, report crimes for small rewards |
| 2 | Night Patroller | Stop NPC muggings, gain Reputation with locals |
| 3 | Bounty Hunter | Claim **bounties** on players (no warrant needed, but must be posted) |
| 4 | Masked Vigilante | Choose an identity + costume (cosmetics); **secret identity** mechanic |
| 5 | Protector | Protect a block — players there get a small defense bonus |
| 6 | Hunter | Track the most-wanted list; take on gang leaders |
| 7 | Legend | City-wide reputation; NPCs bring you tips |
| 8 | Symbol | Seasonal title; your symbol appears on the city map |

**Twist:** Vigilantes are *also* criminals in the eyes of the law — they build Heat when
seen. Police can arrest them. Keeping your **secret identity** (Agility, Perception) is part
of the gameplay.

### Planned later branches

- **Private Investigator** — sells information to every side.
- **Journalist** — exposes corruption; can raise Suspicion on corrupt law players.

## 5. Clean vs. corrupt

Every Law branch has the same ranks, but **Integrity** shapes how you play them:

| | Clean | Corrupt |
|---|---|---|
| Income | Steady salary + legit bonuses | Salary + bribes, protection money, seized goods "lost" |
| Speed | Slower money | Faster money |
| Risk | Very low | Builds **Suspicion** → Internal Affairs investigation → demotion, jail, loss of badge |
| Perks | Public trust, NPC tips, cheaper warrants, unique story ending | Access to black-market gear, street contacts, criminal allies |
| Story | Uncovers The Verdict through evidence | Becomes part of the conspiracy — or uses it |

Neither side is "correct" — it's a choice players make every week.

## 6. The player-driven justice loop

This is where the Law side connects every player in the city:

```
 Street player commits crimes → Heat rises
            ▼
 Detective investigates (Perception) → builds Evidence
            ▼
 Judge (player or NPC) approves Warrant
            ▼
 Officer attempts Arrest ──► Criminal can: fight back / flee / bribe
            ▼ (arrest succeeds)
 Trial: Prosecutor vs. Defense Attorney (player or NPC)
            ▼
 Judge sets sentence within legal range
            ▼
 Criminal goes to Jail (timer) — can be bailed out by crew
```

Every step **pays the player who does it** — so the Law economy is fed by the Street
economy, and vice versa (criminals pay lawyers, cops take bribes, etc.).

**NPCs fill every role when no player is available.** This is important: the loop works on
day one with few players and still works later with thousands.

### Anti-griefing rules

- Arrests need a **warrant** and a minimum **Heat** level — cops cannot arrest clean players.
- Evidence **decays** over time — no holding files forever.
- **False arrest** (case dismissed) lowers Integrity and raises Suspicion.
- Cooldown on arresting the same player.
- Level bands apply to arrests the same as attacks.
- Judges can only sentence within the city's legal range.

## 7. Balance: Law vs. Street

| | Street | Law |
|---|---|---|
| Money | High peaks, high variance | Steady salary, lower peaks |
| Combat | Attack anyone in level band | Arrest only players with Heat + warrant (vigilantes: bounties only) |
| Protection | Bank, crew, housing | Badge: attacking a law player raises your Heat a lot |
| Risk | Jail, being mugged | Internal Affairs (if corrupt), retaliation from gangs |
| Group play | Crews, turf wars | Squads, precincts, law firms |
| Unique power | Raw strength & wealth | Shape the rules: warrants, sentences, city laws |

Balance targets (to validate in testing): over a 30-day period, a Street player and a
Law player of equal activity should end up with roughly equal **total power**
(money + stats + rank). Law should be **deeper**, not **stronger**.

## 8. Assessments — knowing your hidden stats

Assessments are **rare, tied to the story, and never on demand**. There is no coach you
can visit any time.

| Path | Assessor | When it's available | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bum phase | **Doc Mercy** (free clinic) | Only after you've been hospitalized; once per story chapter | Toughness, Strength |
| Police | **Academy Exam** | Only at a promotion | Strength, Agility, Perception |
| Courts | **Bar Exam / Case Review** | Only at a promotion | Wits, Charisma |
| Vigilante | **The Mirror** (story moment) | Only at rank-up story chapters | Integrity, Nerve |
| Street | **Old Sal**, fight-pit promoter | Appears randomly at night; very expensive | Strength, Nerve |

Rules:
- Reveals **1–3 stats**, never all.
- Shows a **tier band compared to your district** (e.g. *"Top 20% in Southside"*), never a number.
- **Accuracy depends on Perception** — low Perception gives a vaguer read.
- Minimum 7-day cooldown between any two assessments.
