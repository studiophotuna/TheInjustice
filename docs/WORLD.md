# The World — 4 Countries, 1 Conspiracy

> All names are fictional and original. No real countries, cities or brands.

## 1. Is 4 countries feasible?

**Yes, if they are phased in and built from data.**

- **Viable:** travel between countries gives long-term goals, trade routes, smuggling,
  different laws per country (great for the Law path) and fresh content without
  making the starting city overwhelming.
- **Risk 1: content cost.** Each country needs its own districts, jobs, NPCs, art and
  story. Doing all 4 at launch would multiply the work by 4.
- **Risk 2: players spread too thin.** With few players, 4 countries feel empty.
  NPCs fill the gaps, and some systems (chat, leaderboards, crews) are global.

**Plan:** launch with **one big country** (the starting city), then release the other
three as **expansions**, each one a story season. Countries are defined as data
(districts, jobs, laws, prices), so adding one is mostly content work, not new code.

## 2. The four countries

The people who framed you (§ The Verdict in [LAW_PATH.md](LAW_PATH.md)) are a
cross-border network called **The Ledger**. Each country holds part of the truth.

### 🌧️ Varrow — the starting country
- **Capital: Grayhaven** — a huge rain-soaked port city of old money and rust.
- **Feel:** 90s noir crime, the classic rags-to-riches city.
- **Laws:** average sentences; courts are slow and **easily bought**.
- **Specialty:** dockside smuggling, unions, crooked courts.
- **Story role:** where you were framed. The Ledger's local face: a respected judge.
- **Release:** launch.

### ☀️ Solmarra — the pleasure coast
- **Capital: Port Solace** — beaches, casinos, yachts and nightlife.
- **Feel:** bright, flashy, sunny on top and rotten underneath.
- **Laws:** **lax and cheap to bribe**; light sentences; police for sale.
- **Specialty:** gambling, money laundering, Hustlers thrive. Easy for corrupt law players.
- **Story role:** where The Ledger hides and washes its money.
- **Release:** expansion 1.

### ❄️ Kestrova — the iron state
- **Capital: Ironmoor** — a cold, fortified city of factories and checkpoints.
- **Feel:** strict, grey, watched.
- **Laws:** **harsh sentences, very hard to bribe**, brutal police. Clean law players
  are strongest here; criminals pay huge prices on the black market.
- **Specialty:** weapons, contraband, prison breaks, underground fight pits.
- **Story role:** where The Ledger's enforcers are trained.
- **Release:** expansion 2.

### 🌐 Meridian — the glass republic
- **Capital: Halcyon** — towers, corporations and the richest people in the world.
- **Feel:** clean, futuristic, corporate.
- **Laws:** **the rich are above the law**: white-collar crime is barely punished,
  street crime is punished hard.
- **Specialty:** hacking, fraud, corporate law, high-end heists.
- **Story role:** home of The Ledger's leaders. The final chapter.
- **Release:** expansion 3.

## 3. Grayhaven — a bigger city

Ten districts, unlocked by rank, Reputation or story progress:

| # | District | Vibe | Unlocks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Gutter** | Slums under the overpass, where you start | Everyone (bum phase) |
| 2 | **Old Market** | Street stalls, pawn shops, cheap food | Everyone |
| 3 | **Iron Row** | Factories and warehouses | Labor jobs, Enforcers, security guards |
| 4 | **The Docks** | Cargo, smuggling, unions | Thieves, smugglers, Narcotics detectives |
| 5 | **Southside** | Rough neighborhoods, gangs and turf | Street crews, Vigilantes |
| 6 | **Neon Strip** | Clubs, bars, back-room card games | Hustlers |
| 7 | **Precinct Square** | Police HQ and the academy | Police path |
| 8 | **Courthouse Hill** | Courts, law firms, city hall | Courts path |
| 9 | **Midtown** | Offices and banks | Fraud, heists, high-rank law players |
| 10 | **Lantern Heights** | Mansions of the elite | Late game; The Ledger's local base |

Each district has its own job list, shops, NPCs and crime level. Later, crews can
fight over **turf** in Southside, the Docks and the Neon Strip.

## 4. Travel between countries

- Travel by bus, ferry, train or plane. Cheaper options are slower.
- Travel takes **real time** (a timer), costs money and needs papers (a passport,
  or a forged one, which raises Heat if caught).
- You can only fight, arrest or trade with people **in the same country**.
- **Smuggling:** buy goods cheap in one country and sell them in another, with a
  risk of customs checks (Perception and Wits help).
- **Law players** can work in another country's police or courts only after
  transferring their badge or license (a story quest).
- **Heat is per country**: being wanted in Kestrova doesn't make you wanted in Solmarra.
